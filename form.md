### What you built *
*(Max 6000 characters)*
```text
Pip’s Midnight Nursery is an original, fully interactive storytelling website designed to introduce Pip, the official Junior Dream Engineer of Cloud 9½, to the haX.baby universe. Rather than building a static portfolio, we designed an evolving bedside sanctuary where every pixel reacts to the visitor's curiosity.

Key areas of the experience:

1. Retro Bios Boot Screen:
The website awakens with an authentic phosphor CRT system boot sequence ("MIDNIGHT NURSERY BIOS v2.04"), authenticating sleep compliance and calibrating dream frequencies before gently transitioning into the nursery.

2. Living Hero Sanctuary & Eye Tracking:
At the heart of Cloud 9½ sits Pip. Using responsive vector trigonometry, Pip’s large glowing eyes follow the visitor's cursor across the screen with natural damping and soft parallax depth. Visitors can click on Pip to hear his gentle spoken thoughts, accompanied by a dynamic typewriter dialogue bubble.

3. The Secret Silver Key (Dual-Personality Shift):
Tucked upon Pip’s chest ribbon is an enchanted silver key. Clicking it initiates a seamless, full-site transformation:
- Dream Mode (Pastel Palette): Soft pinks, fluffy clouds, twinkling starlight, and comforting bedtime blessings.
- Nightmare Shift (Midnight Palette): Deep crimson embers, swirling purple auras, glowing red X-eyes, and mischievous night-shift dialogue.
Clicking the key again safely returns the nursery back to sweet dreams without intrusive popups.

4. Interactive 3D Unicorn Music Box:
A custom 3D music box built with Three.js that visitors can spin and inspect in 360-degree orbit. Visitors can click the music box to wind the spring and play an authentic, relaxing 3-minute lullaby composed with gentle womb and ambient starlight tones.

5. Interactive Nursery Nightlights:
A curated gallery of protective nightlights (Starlight Jar, Cloud Lantern, Lavender Moon, and the Forbidden Ember). Dreamers can toggle them, inspect their bedtime descriptions, and bookmark their favorite bedside light.

6. The Starlight Dream Delivery Console:
An interactive dream dispatch station where visitors pick a recipient (Sleepy Kittens, Worried Coders, Stargazers), adjust dream ingredients (Lavender, Moonbeams, Cloud Fluff), and launch a custom dream capsule across the cosmos with live dispatch receipts.

7. Starlight Wish Pillow (Guestbook):
A private community wish bank that saves bedtime thoughts and dream wishes locally in the visitor's browser memory (LocalStorage) with instant emoji reactions and real-time deletion/preservation.

8. Dream Capsule Launcher (Hackathon Special Surprise):
Located in the closing section, clicking "Launch Dream Capsule" fires a sequence of glowing, color-shifting dream orbs across the viewport with physics-based particle trajectories, followed by a skull-and-star confetti shower that reveals a personalized, collectible bedtime prophecy card from Pip.
```

## 04. Tell the Story Behind It

### Your approach to the challenge *
*(Max 4000 characters)*
```text
For haX.baby Track 1 ("Official Pip"), our goal was to reach Boss Level / Tier 3 Legendary. We rejected the idea of a standard developer portfolio in favor of a cohesive interactive playground where Pip feels truly alive.

Pip has two contrasting canonical aspects: his sweet, sleepy daytime role as Junior Dream Engineer, and his mischievous, horned Nightmare Pip persona who delivers curious nightmares to the unruly. Our core creative philosophy was to make this contrast fluid, discoverable, and tactile.

We made deliberate design choices:
1. Zero Framework Bloat: Built entirely with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JS so that any visitor on mobile or desktop can load the entire universe instantly with zero build step, no npm overhead, and zero third-party tracking.
2. Emotional Immersion: Every action has sensory feedback—from the gentle typewriter sound of Pip's speech to responsive eye tracking, musical tines, and glowing ember animations.
3. Respecting the Lore: Nightmare Pip is mischievous and edgy, but never scary or gory. He is Pip’s nocturnal work shift, maintaining dream balance in the cosmos.
```

### Challenges & learnings (Optional)
*(Max 4000 characters)*
```text
One key challenge was implementing cursor eye tracking on a responsive character illustration. Because the character scales dynamically across mobile and desktop breakpoints, hardcoded pixel coordinates would cause the eyes to drift off Pip’s face. We solved this by binding normalized trigonometric offsets relative to the bounding box of Pip's face, ensuring perfect ocular symmetry at any resolution.

Another rewarding challenge was creating a seamless full-site theme transition that didn't feel jarring. Instead of an abrupt CSS class switch, we orchestrated a multi-stage sequence: Pip shivers, title glyphs subtly glitch, ambient mist deepens, and background gradients interpolate into midnight crimson, giving judges a cinematic reveal that feels natural and magical.
```
